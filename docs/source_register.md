Source-table audit: 2026-11-09; OSRM route retrieval: 2026-11-10 UTC. Record-level extraction was completed for the Korean government dataset and Korea Foodbank map. **Update (2026-11-18):** The 2025 Seoul operating-statistics table has now been cross-checked against the directory: 31 of 32 entries match by exact Korean name; the national-level `전국푸드뱅크` entry has no separate Seoul row. This supersedes the earlier 20-site count above. The March 2025 Mapo receipt-ledger PDF was also inspected, superseding the earlier statement that its attachments had not been audited. It documents dated item receipts and prices, but quantities are not consistently weights and do not show spare capacity. Donor names are masked and were not copied into project data.
